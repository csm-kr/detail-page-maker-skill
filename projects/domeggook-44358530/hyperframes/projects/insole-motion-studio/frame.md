# Frame direction

## Visual system

- Background: warm charcoal `#181612`, paper `#F5F0E8`
- Accent: heat orange `#FF7A1A`, muted orange `#D95F13`
- Text: paper `#F5F0E8`, ink `#242019`, muted `#BFB5A7`
- Caution: brick `#C54C3D`
- Typeface: Noto Sans KR / Malgun Gothic
- Corners: 24–34px, 1px warm translucent border, restrained shadow

## Composition

- Headline in the upper 138px safe area.
- Evidence or product stage from y=150 to y=690.
- Evidence note in the lower 72px safe area.
- A scene has one dominant product image, one primary data motion, and one ambient layer.
- No decorative motion may cross a number, range endpoint, or product boundary annotation.

## Motion

- 0.00–0.70s: headline and stage establish.
- 0.70–3.50s: one claim is demonstrated.
- 3.50–4.65s: result holds with a slow local glow or settled control.
- 4.65–5.20s: gentle exit or stable end frame.
- All timelines are seek-safe, finite, and use explicit `fromTo` states.
